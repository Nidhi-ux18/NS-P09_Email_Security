ABC Training Institute
Project: NS-P09 Email Domain Security
Scenario: Two-Campus Training Institute

Domain:
abctraining.edu

DMARC Policy:

v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@abctraining.edu

Policy Explanation:

p=none
Monitor email authentication results without rejecting messages.

p=quarantine
Unauthenticated or misaligned emails may be treated as suspicious
or placed in spam/quarantine.

p=reject
Unauthenticated or misaligned emails should be rejected.

DMARC Alignment:

SPF Alignment:
The SPF authenticated domain should align with the visible From domain.

DKIM Alignment:
The DKIM signing domain should align with the visible From domain.

Recommended Deployment:

1. Start with p=none for monitoring.
2. Analyse SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
3. Move to p=quarantine after validation.
4. Move to p=reject when legitimate mail is confirmed.
