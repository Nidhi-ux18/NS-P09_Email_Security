DMARC Policy Stages

p=none
Monitoring mode. Emails are not blocked.
p=quarantine
Suspicious or failed emails are treated as potentially unsafe.
p=reject
Failed authentication is rejected according to the DMARC policy.

DMARC Alignment

DMARC checks whether the domain authenticated by SPF or DKIM aligns with the visible From domain.

SPF Alignment: SPF authenticated domain matches the From domain.

DKIM Alignment: DKIM signing domain matches the From domain.
