ABC Training Institute
Project: NS-P09 Email Domain Security
Scenario: Two-Campus Training Institute

Domain:
abctraining.edu

SPF Record:
v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all

Purpose:
Allow authorized Google mail servers to send emails for
the institute domain used by both Campus A and Campus B.

SPF Components:

v=spf1
Identifies this as an SPF version 1 record.

include:_spf.google.com
Authorizes Google's mail servers to send email for
abctraining.edu.

~all
SoftFail for other unauthorized sending servers.
These messages should be treated as suspicious and
reviewed by the receiving mail system.

Two-Campus Implementation:

Campus A - Main Campus
Uses the institute's authorized Google mail service.

Campus B - Branch Campus
Uses the same authorized Google mail service.

Security Objective:
Only authorized mail infrastructure should send email
using the abctraining.edu domain.

Testing:
SPF PASS = Authorized Google mail server.

SPF FAIL / SOFTFAIL = Sender is not authorized by the
SPF policy and requires further investigation.
