ABC Training Institute
Project: NS-P09 Email Domain Security
Scenario: Two-Campus Training Institute

SPOOFING CASE ANALYSIS

Scenario:
An unauthorized sender attempts to send an email pretending to be
from the ABC Training Institute domain.

Visible From Domain:
abctraining.edu

SPF Check:
The unauthorized sending server is not authorized by the SPF policy.
Therefore, SPF may result in FAIL or SOFTFAIL.

DKIM Check:
The unauthorized email does not have a valid DKIM signature from
the authorized institute domain.
Therefore, DKIM may result in FAIL.

DMARC Check:
If SPF and DKIM authentication/alignment do not satisfy DMARC,
the message fails DMARC.

DMARC Policy Response:

p=none:
The message is monitored and authentication results are recorded.

p=quarantine:
The suspicious message may be placed in spam/quarantine.

p=reject:
The suspicious unauthenticated or misaligned message should be
rejected.

Security Benefit:
SPF identifies authorized sending servers.
DKIM verifies the authorized digital signature.
DMARC checks authentication and domain alignment and applies
the configured policy.

Conclusion:
Using SPF, DKIM and DMARC together reduces the risk of successful
email-domain spoofing against the training institute.
