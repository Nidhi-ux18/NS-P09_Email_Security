ABC Training Institute

Two-Campus Email Security Scenario

NS-P09 - SPF, DKIM and DMARC

Project Scenario

ABC Training Institute operates through two campuses: Main Campus and Branch Campus.

Both campuses use the common email domain: training-institute.local

The email security system protects communication between the two campuses and helps identify unauthorized or spoofed email messages.

Two-Campus Environment

Main Campus

Role: Main Training Campus

Email Domain: training-institute.local

Authorized staff use the institute email domain for communication.

Branch Campus

Role: Branch Training Campus

Email Domain: training-institute.local

Authorized staff communicate with the Main Campus using institute email.

Email Security Controls

SPF: Checks whether the sending mail server is authorized for the domain.

DKIM: Verifies the digital signature associated with the email.

DMARC: Checks authentication and domain alignment and applies the domain policy.

Email Header Analysis: Examines SPF, DKIM and DMARC authentication results from email headers.
